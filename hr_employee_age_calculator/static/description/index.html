Employee Age Overview

Instantly View Employee Age in HR Module

Simplify HR Management

This module enhances the Employee form by automatically showing each employee’s age based on their date of birth. It helps HR managers quickly view, compare, and analyze employee ages without manual calculation.

  • Auto-calculated Age — Computed directly from Date of Birth.
  • Visible in Employee Form — Displayed clearly for easy reference.
  • No Configuration Needed — Works instantly after installation.

Keep your HR data more informative and actionable — let Odoo calculate the age for you.
