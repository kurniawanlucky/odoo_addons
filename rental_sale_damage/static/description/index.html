Sale ↔ Rental Damage Bridge

Integrate Sales Orders with Rental Damage Reports

This module links Sale Orders with Rental Damage Reports from the rental_product_damage_tracker module. It helps your team record and track any damage found on rental items (e.g., cars, equipment) directly from the corresponding Sale Order.

Key Features

  • Adds a smart button on Sale Orders to view related damage reports.
  • Allows creating new Rental Damage Reports directly from a Sale Order.
  • Automatically links damage reports to the Sale Order and rental product.
  • Displays the Sale Order on the Damage Report form and tree view.
  • Supports Odoo chatter and activity tracking for follow-up.

How It Works

  1. Open any Sale Order that includes rental products.
  2. Click the Damage Reports smart button.
  3. View or create damage reports linked to that order.
  4. Each report tracks findings, severity, images, and repair status.

Example Workflow

Customer rents a car (Sale Order created)
During return, staff creates a Damage Report from that Sale Order
The report is linked automatically, showing affected parts and photos
Once fixed, the state updates to Closed
